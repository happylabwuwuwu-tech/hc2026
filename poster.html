❓ Q1. 臨床試驗做過了嗎？有沒有長者改善的數據？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"The software development and service architecture are completed, but the facility-based clinical evaluation has not yet been conducted. In this paper, we report the workflow-aware service design, implementation principles, and the 12-week evaluation protocol under IRB approval NYCU112062AF."
📝 應答心法 (GAMMA 誠實準則)：千萬不要為了好看而捏造臨床療效或宣稱「已經證明有效」。誠實說明本篇是報告「系統開發、服務設計與前瞻臨床評估研究計畫」。
❓ Q2. 什麼是學習療法（Learning Therapy）？它跟一般平板遊戲有何不同？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"Learning Therapy was pioneered by Dr. Kawashima in Japan, using structured oral reading and basic arithmetic to stimulate frontal brain regions. Unlike individual entertainment apps, our service is workflow-aware: it is built specifically for facility caregivers to lead small groups on a shared screen, eliminating manual worksheet preparation and isolated tablet use."
📝 應答心法：強調不是丟給長者單獨玩 iPad，而是「照護員帶領小團體」的工作流整合。
❓ Q3. 為什麼堅持「小團體共用大螢幕」，而不是讓長者一人一台平板？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"In institutional dementia care, many residents have motor, sensory, or visual limitations that make independent tablet interaction frustrating. A shared display guided by staff fosters interpersonal social interactions, prevents isolation, and allows the caregiver to pace the session safely."
📝 應答心法：點出機構長者的身體限制（手部顫抖、視力退化）與團體社交帶來的正面心理效益。
❓ Q4. 你們強調的「臺灣在地化文化內容」具體指什麼？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"Translated foreign materials often lack personal relevance for Taiwanese seniors. We incorporated culturally familiar proverbs, traditional Taiwanese holidays, local landmarks, and nostalgic life experiences into our reading and verbal fluency modules, which stimulates autobiographical memory and spontaneous participation."
📝 應答心法：說明本土文化記憶能喚起自傳式記憶 (autobiographical memory)，提升開口與參與動機。
❓ Q5. 長者的隱私與機構資料安全如何控管？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"All resident activity logs are linked to de-identified study IDs with role-restricted permissions. No identifiable health records reside on the client screen, complying with institutional data governance and our approved IRB protocol."
📝 應答心法：去識別化 (De-identified) 與角色權限控管 (Role-restricted)，符合 IRB 規範。
❓ Q6. 12 週試驗預計採用什麼統計模型分析？為什麼不用傳統 t 檢定？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"We plan to use baseline-adjusted Linear Mixed-Effects Models (LMM) or Generalized Estimating Equations (GEE). Because measurements are taken longitudinally within group cohorts, LMM and GEE properly handle repeated within-subject correlation and group clustering, which simple t-tests cannot accommodate."
📝 應答心法：展現對長期追蹤 (longitudinal repeated measures) 與群體聚集效應 (clustering) 的統計嚴謹度。
❓ Q7. 本篇論文對 IEEE HealthCom 的最大貢獻是什麼？
💡 建議回答 (English):
"Our primary contribution is bridging the gap between digital health technology and real-world caregiving workflow. Rather than adding administrative burden, our app co-designs reusable group modules, automated session documentation, and cultural adaptation to enable sustainable eHealth deployment in dementia care."
📝 應答心法：點題「Workflow-Aware eHealth Service」——這正是 HealthCom 研討會最看重的落地實用價值。
